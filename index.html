Revenue trajectory

2.3x revenue in six years · 14.8% compound annual growth since FY2019

In plain English: the jump in 2022 is not organic growth. S&P Global merged with IHS Markit in February 2022, which nearly doubled the size of the company overnight. Everything after that is the combined business growing steadily on its own.

Where the revenue comes from

FY2025 revenue by division, USD millions

In plain English: five businesses. Market Intelligence sells data and software subscriptions. Ratings grades the creditworthiness of debt. Energy sells commodity price benchmarks. Indices owns the S&P 500 and earns fees when funds track it. Mobility sold car data — it was spun off in July 2026.

Earnings per share

GAAP vs adjusted diluted EPS, USD

Why two numbers: GAAP EPS follows the accounting rulebook and includes one-off items such as the amortisation of intangibles from the IHS Markit merger. Adjusted EPS strips those out to show underlying trading. The gap between the two is closing as merger accounting rolls off. The FY2022 adjusted figure is on a pro forma basis.

The story to watch

S&P Global is now a four-division company

    What a spin-off means: Mobility was not sold for cash. Shareholders were handed shares in a brand-new listed company, Mobility Global. S&P Global keeps four divisions and roughly $1.7 billion of annual revenue leaves with Mobility — which is why the 2026 comparisons are shown "pro forma", meaning as if the split had already happened.
